# Game features (ideas list, 19 Nov 2026)

Every feature idea the owner has had, in one place. Status: **idea** means
noted, not approved yet. Each one gets its own card, approved one at a time
(like the blocks), and then its status becomes **approved**. Blocks are in
`docs/WORLD-RULES.md`.

## A. Travel and home

1. **Watch door** (approved 19 Nov): a shortcut to islands and minigames
   with no menus or loading screens. Open the watch, pick a place or
   Start minigame → pick a game; a plain door (no glow) appears; he walks up,
   opens it and walks through. Goes to other islands, his home island, or the
   gate right by the chosen minigame. The destination is already visible
   through the open door (a portal; kept light for the old MacBook). Works
   indoors and outdoors; in a tight spot a message says "Door needs space".
   Bought with coins or won as a prize; lost when he dies (buy or win again).
2. **Home island** (approved 19 Nov): his own place to store stuff and
   build. Unlocked by playing (how much: open). Starts small and plain
   (grass, dirt, bedrock). The one place he can add and break blocks
   (rulebook blocks only; must pass the world checker). Stored items are
   safe when he dies. More coins = more he can do: buy land to expand it,
   build his own tracks and skateparks from blocks, and buy ready-made game
   kits for fun (bowling alley, hoop, pool table; a home track comes with a
   start/finish kit). Players cannot design their own minigame rules.
   He reaches it through the watch door or from the menu screen. Friends:
   invited through an option in the watch; other players can only enter
   through his door. Size limit: decided later, maybe by device.
3. **Free-roam driving** (idea): karts drive on any ground they can reach;
   each block's grip decides how much they slide.
4. **Swimming travel** (idea): a lazy river, partly through tunnels, to get
   around the islands.

16. **Big connected world** (idea): islands linked by walking bridges and
    connecting roads; every game can be reached by walking or driving.

## B. Money and items

5. **Coins** (approved 19 Nov): the reward for playing. Earned by winning
   minigames (more for 1st place, some just for playing), maybe small amounts
   hidden around the world. Spent on shop items, the watch door, home-island
   land, game kits and upgrades. Always kept when he dies (only carried items
   are at risk). No real money for now. Shown in a screen corner and the watch.
17. **Prizes** (approved 19 Nov): sometimes after winning a minigame, more
    often for 1st place. Items (skateboard, watch door, cosmetics like colours
    and outfits), game kits, rarely home-island land. Matched to the game
    (golf prizes lean golf, racing prizes lean racing). A duplicate turns into
    coins. A short prize reveal after the win.
6. **Shop** (approved 19 Nov): opened from the watch, plus a few shop
   buildings around the world. Sells rides (skateboard, bike, scooter), the
   watch door, sports gear (clubs, balls, bat, cue), cosmetics, home-island
   land and game kits. Items lost on death show first for easy rebuying.
   Prices set later. Bought items go straight into his item pull-out.
7. **Item pull-out** (approved 19 Nov, like GTA): hold a button, an item
   wheel appears, pick an item and it is in his hands. Holds everything he
   owns and carries (rides, sports gear, the watch door). Works anywhere,
   e.g. a golf club and ball to hit from any block, even a cloud. One item
   in hand at a time. Not during minigames (they give him the gear).
8. **Losing loot when you die** (idea): decide later.

## C. Riding

9. **Skateboarding** (idea): ride, jump, drop off edges onto ramps.
10. **Bikes and scooters** (idea).

## D. Moving around

11. **Ledge grab and climbing** (idea): grab edges and hang; climb almost
    any solid block, trees and leaves too. Movement step after jumping.
12. **Clouds and secret ramps** (idea): hard-to-reach clouds; grab the edge,
    pull up, then drop off (e.g. on a skateboard) onto secret ramps.

## E. Messing around

13. **Hits** (idea, remember): punch or kick other players or NPCs and they
    stumble and fall; throw a ball at anyone; whack someone with a pool cue.
    Not a boxing game. Needs balance first.

## F. People

14. **Online players and NPCs** (idea): other players and running NPCs to
    throw at, race and mess around with.

## G. Objects (after blocks)

15. **Objects list** (idea): turn arrow signs first, then hoops, pins,
    targets. Signs and hoops are built from small concrete blocks and paint;
    balls, pins and other moving objects are not made of blocks.
