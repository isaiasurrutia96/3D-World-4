# Avatar movement direction

The avatar should express high **bodily-kinesthetic intelligence**: athletic coordination, proprioception, balance and deliberate control of weight. This is the owner's direction for all future motion work.

- Every step places the foot deliberately and transfers weight convincingly over support.
- The body anticipates turns, stops, landings and changes of direction; it should feel agile and capable.
- Hips, knees, ankles, torso and arms work together. Avoid a clumsy shuffle, uncontrolled knee pumping, sliding, or an unbalanced upper body.
- Carrying and sports motions should show an athlete's command of their body and the object, while keeping the accepted avatar shape and proportions.

Judge animation by what it looks and feels like as well as numerical contact checks. The universal Test Lab is the workspace for comparing views, inspecting slow motion and trying controls. This direction does not imply approval of the current walking/running tests.
